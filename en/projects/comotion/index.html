CoMotion

An interactive system for steering text-to-motion generation with action-level controls.

Why action-level controls?

A prompt can describe several actions at once. CoMotion gives people controls for the actions represented in the system so they can adjust the emphasis of a specific movement while refining a generated sequence.

Semantic steering pipeline

The system matches the text to atomic motion concepts and exposes the matches as adjustable controls. After the first generation, an action-level setting guides replacement of selected tokens in a discrete latent sequence. A decoder reconstructs the updated motion for inspection.

Interaction and comparison

Users enter a prompt, adjust the strengths of matched actions, and compare generated motions in a three-view interface. The demo also lets them save a result.

Evaluation

The model evaluation used 500 prompt–target motion pairs and examined latent alignment, diversity, and FID. In a separate within-participant study, 12 participants compared two conditions across five tasks. Task time covered generation, network latency, and user interaction. Condition order and learning effects matter when interpreting the comparisons.
