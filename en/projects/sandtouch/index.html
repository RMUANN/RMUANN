SandTouch

A VR sand painting system with hand gestures, realistic sound, and contextual AI guidance.

Design problem

Physical sand painting requires specialized equipment and takes practice. SandTouch explores how to make the medium accessible in VR while preserving the gestures and feedback that shape the experience.

Interaction and system

Hand tracking and gesture recognition let users manipulate virtual sand directly. Sound accompanies the interaction, and an AI agent interprets ongoing activity to offer contextually relevant artistic suggestions.

Evaluation

The CHI study reports increased engagement and immersion with the system and examines how realistic sound affects the painting experience and users’ reported emotional relief.
