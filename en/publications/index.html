Research & Publications

How can people express what they want from a generative system, adjust its behavior, and judge the result?

Research directions

Controllable text-to-motion

Use language to describe a motion and give people a way to tune its constituent actions.

Interactive motion editing

Make the effect of an edit visible through controls, playback, and side-by-side comparisons.

Human–computer interaction

Study how people express intent, evaluate generated results, and decide when to intervene.

Immersive creative tools

Use gestures and sound to support hands-on creative work in virtual environments.

Agent-assisted interaction

Explore how agents infer intent and provide context-sensitive guidance in creative and task-oriented workflows.
